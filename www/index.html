About 3D Viewer

Supported formats:

  • GLB / glTF (full materials & textures)
  • FBX (meshes + embedded textures)
  • VRM (avatars with MToon materials)
  • STL (geometry; vertex colors if present)
  • OBJ (geometry; vertex colors if present)
